# P0 — Add energy accounting and standard SAR metrics

Labels: `P0`, `analysis`, `paper-1`

## Goal

Separate complex amplitude, integrated power and display normalization, then report target ROI energy, leakage, PSLR, ISLR and resolution.

## Acceptance

- A defined unmodulated energy reference and non-overlapping ROIs.
- Theoretical harmonic power budget compared with raw-data/RD measurements.
- Conservation residual and out-of-window energy are reported.
- Metrics are not inferred from PNG brightness.
